How this is calculated / assumptions used
  • Vintage (M0–M6): WRP & Active Advisor targets follow the joining-vintage table exactly as issued (SAP 2.0 FY27 Q1).
  • Post M6: WRP & Advisor targets switch to the monthly seasonality table (Apr–Mar), repeating each fiscal year.
  • License Active: simplified to a flat 2 per month from M1 onward (reaches the 24 annual target in 12 months), per your instruction.
  • BA Confirmation: simplified to a flat 1 per month from M1 onward (reaches the 12 annual target in 12 months), per your instruction.
  • Score: %Achievement per category is capped at 150%, then multiplied by its weight (WRP 50%, Advisor 20%, License 20%, BA 10%).
  • Promotion rule: Total score ≥ 100% and the 13th-month persistency gate ≥ 80% (once applicable). Adjust in code if your actual cutoff differs.
  • All manager-band targets (ABM B5 → CBM) use the "DT" target column, which is what the monthly seasonality sheets are built on.
